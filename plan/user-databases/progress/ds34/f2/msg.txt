Don't hold a worker waiting on a store's history export lock

An export queued while another of the store runs (a backfill can take minutes,
while every turn end and the minutely sweep queue more) waited on
pg_advisory_lock, holding an app worker, so ingest stalled for everyone. It now
tries the lock and leaves the store to the running export; what's left pending,
the sweep queues again. Deleting the exported history still must not race an
export, so it waits up to LOCK_WAIT seconds, then answers busy (503).
